Collected 28/10/2026 for the Omini Platform 11/2026 plan. **Facebook blocked logged-out
access**, so post feeds, cadence and per-post engagement were NOT measured. Patterns come
from competitor websites, blogs and press. Follower counts are undated search snippets.
Refresh with a logged-in review or Meta Ad Library before relying on numbers.

## Tactics that transfer to Omini

1. Recurring, named series on a fixed rhythm (Buymed's weekly lives).
2. Explain a new regulation within days, in carousel form: "Áp dụng từ / Nhà thuốc cần làm gì / Omini hỗ trợ gì" (MISA, KiotViet). **Always check the original document first.**
3. Bring in an outside expert (accountant, pharmacist, doctor) for lives.
4. Free plan stated in exact numbers, never just "miễn phí".
5. Customer stories with a fixed structure and two hard numbers (Sapo).
6. Guide track for people about to open a pharmacy (TLGo); this audience hasn't chosen software yet.
7. Giveaways tied to a real action, with winners announced live (Buymed). Only after legal approval.

## Gaps nobody fills (Omini can own)

- Short demos of daily liên thông and closing the day ("chốt ca, đẩy dữ liệu" in 60 s).
- Sổ quỹ / S2e explained specifically for pharmacy HKD.
- Owner-to-owner voice: polls, honest posts, light humour. Every competitor sounds institutional.
- A simple self-check for being compliant.
